Privacy Policy

RA Rideway Private Hire respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection law.

When you enquire about or book a journey, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, pickup and destination details, journey date and time, and any additional information you provide.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange and manage journeys, communicate with customers, maintain necessary business records and meet legal obligations.

Payment Policy

RA Rideway Private Hire accepts online card payments only. Cash payments are not accepted.

Customers will be informed of the total fare before confirming and paying for their booking.

Card payments must be completed through an authorised secure payment service.

Booking confirmation will be provided once payment has been successfully processed and the journey accepted.

For payment enquiries, contact 07403 816343.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Customers may cancel their booking by contacting RA Rideway Private Hire.

48 hours or more before pickup: Full refund.

24 to less than 48 hours before pickup: 50% refund.

Less than 24 hours before pickup: No refund.

No-shows: No refund where the customer fails to attend the agreed pickup location.

Operator cancellations: If RA Rideway cancels a booking, the customer receives a full refund.

Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 working days, depending on the payment provider.

These terms do not affect customers' statutory rights under applicable UK law.

Cancellation enquiries: 07403 816343.
